Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information PolyMath Precision, LLC ("PolyMath Precision", "we", "us") handles through this website, polymathprecision.com, and through VAULT, our software that can connect to a Google Drive account. We keep it short because we collect very little.

The website

We don't collect personal information through this site. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

  • Project form. The "Prepare project brief" form runs entirely in your browser. It builds a text file on your own device for you to download and share with us yourself. Nothing you type is sent to us or stored by us.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load. See Google's privacy policy.

VAULT and Google Drive

VAULT is a self-hosted application. It runs on its owner's own server, not on a service operated by PolyMath Precision. VAULT can connect to a Google Drive account so the owner can browse and search their own Drive files from inside VAULT.

  • Access requested. VAULT asks Google only for read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly). It cannot create, change, move or delete anything in your Drive.
  • How the data is used. File names, folders and file contents are read only to show them to the account owner inside their own VAULT, including searching them. They are not used for any other purpose.
  • Where it is stored. The sign-in token Google issues is stored encrypted on the VAULT owner's own server. Files are read from Google Drive when needed and may be cached temporarily on that same server. Nothing is sent to PolyMath Precision or anyone else.
  • Who can see it. Only the signed-in owner. The connected Drive is kept in a private area that other VAULT users and automated agents cannot open.
  • Sharing. We don't sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Removing access. Removing the connection in VAULT deletes its stored sign-in. You can also revoke VAULT's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing or revoking never deletes files in your Drive.

VAULT's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

This site and VAULT are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
